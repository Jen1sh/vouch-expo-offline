## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a high-trust, intentional social ecosystem centered on communal credibility and human endorsement. The aesthetic fuses warm editorial refinement with contemporary product tactility—moving intentionally away from disposable, gamified dating and networking paradigms toward considered introductions and institutional warmth.

The target audience comprises discerning professionals, creators, and community members seeking verified social capital, meaningful personal connections, and accountability. Every touchpoint prioritizes deliberate interaction over rapid consumption.

### Design Principles & Visual Language
- **Warm Editorial Elegance:** Thoughtful layout rhythms, generous negative space, and distinguished serif typography deliver an editorial, publication-grade tone.
- **Physicality & Tactility:** Cards behave as tangible heavyweight stock surfaces with subtle surface borders, soft diffused ambient drops, and deliberate physics.
- **Role-Aware Presence:** Clear distinction between introspective profile discovery ("Member Mode") and responsible community endorsement ("Voucher Mode") via subtle atmospheric tinting and prominent header badges.
- **Intentional Signals:** Badges, offline queue statuses, and trust indicators are quiet, precise, and authoritative rather than loud or gamified.

## Colors

The palette is anchored in an authoritative deep slate charcoal, balanced against warm stone and cream foundations to evoke tactile paper, bespoke stationery, and quiet confidence.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#0F172A` - Slate Charcoal):** Used for commanding typography, primary structural actions, high-emphasis icons, and structural borders.
- **Secondary (`#D97706` & `#F59E0B` - Deep Warm Amber / Gold):** Represents trust, personal endorsements, mutual connections, vouches, and highlights. Used selectively to denote high-value communal capital.
- **Tertiary (`#059669` / `#10B981` - Heritage Emerald):** Applied strictly to verified profiles, validated credentials, confirmed introductions, and synced offline states.
- **Critical / Divergence (`#E11D48` / `#F43F5E` - Rose / Coral):** Reserved for declensions, skips, revocations, and system alerts, keeping negative actions respectful and deliberate rather than alarming.
- **Neutral Foundations:**
  - `Surface Base`: `#FAFAF9` (Stone 50)
  - `Surface Subdued / Layer 1`: `#F5F5F4` (Stone 100)
  - `Surface Elevated / Cards`: `#FFFFFF` (Pure White)
  - `Border Subtle`: `#E7E5E4` (Stone 200)
  - `Border Strong`: `#D6D3D1` (Stone 300)
  - `Text Primary`: `#0F172A` (Slate 900)
  - `Text Secondary`: `#57534E` (Stone 600)
  - `Text Muted`: `#A8A29E` (Stone 400)

### Mode Color Signatures
- **Member Mode:** Pristine cream surfaces (`#FAFAF9`), deep slate text, and amber accent tags.
- **Voucher Mode:** Subtly warmed substrate (`#FBF8F3`), enriched amber-tinted borders (`#FDE68A`), and a dedicated mode indicator badge featuring `#D97706` accents.

## Typography

The type system creates an immediate contrast between literary gravitas and functional clarity.

- **Editorial Serifs (Newsreader):** Applied to persona quotes, profile headlines, vouch narratives, and welcome moments. It introduces warmth and the prestige of a bespoke printed membership ledger.
- **Contemporary Grotesque (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Powers all functional metadata, metrics, form inputs, button actions, and system statuses. Its precise proportions ensure immediate legibility across compact mobile viewports and cards.
- **Stylistic Guideline:** Long-form testimonials and vouch notes must use `headline-sm` or `body-lg` in *italic* styling when representing direct peer endorsements, establishing an authentic human voice.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts adhere to an 8-point harmonic spacing grid (with 4px increments for tightly spaced atomic components such as chips and indicators).

### Breakpoints & Adaptive Rhythm
- **Mobile (<640px):** Single-column flow with a fixed maximum card container width of `420px`. Card margins are pinned to `16px` (`gutter-mobile`). Actions sit in an ergonomically anchored bottom bar.
- **Tablet (640px - 1024px):** Centered card deck or dual-pane split (queue view on left, vouch verification canvas on right) with `24px` (`gutter-tablet`).
- **Desktop (>1024px):** Fixed max layout width of `1152px` (`max-content-width`). Focus shifts to an intentional three-column workflow: Community Navigation (Left), Active Introduction / Vouch Canvas (Center, `480px` max), and Contextual Social Proof Ledger (Right).

All interactive cards preserve vertical content rhythm with generous padding (`card-inset: 1.25rem`) to guarantee readability without visual crowding.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy combines low-contrast structural borders with warm, ambient lighting. Elements mimic physical heavy-stock paper and linen index cards rather than flat screens.

### Elevation Levels
- **Level 0 (Floor):** Base surfaces (`#FAFAF9` or `#F5F5F4`) without shadows.
- **Level 1 (Structural Containers & List Items):** `#FFFFFF` with a crisp `1px solid #E7E5E4` stroke and a resting shadow: `0 1px 3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 1px 2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Tactile Interactive Cards):** Active card states and swipable vouch elements. Defined by `1px solid #E2E8F0` and deep, warm-tinted ambient diffusion: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Overlays, Modals, & Sheet Menus):** Pure white backdrops with border `1px solid #E7E5E4` accompanied by `0 20px 32px -8px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12), 0 8px 16px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06)`.
- **Level 4 (Floating System Triggers & Badges):** High-priority micro-surfaces (e.g., Offline/Synced pill, Dev Panel trigger) use: `0 10px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.16)`.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a balanced **Rounded (`2`)** curvature philosophy. 

- **Containers & Deck Cards:** Containers use `rounded-xl` (`1.5rem` / `24px`); the swipable **deck/vouch cards** use a softer `rounded-2xl` (`2rem` / `32px`) so the large full-bleed card face never reads as sharp-edged.
- **Interactive Controls & Fields:** Utilize `rounded-lg` (`0.75rem` / `12px`) for buttons, inputs, and preview modules.
- **Badges, Pills, & Status Triggers:** Retain full pill styling (`9999px`) to immediately denote contextual status, role indication, and floating meta-controls.

## Components

### 1. Buttons
- **Primary Action (Endorse / Vouch):** Filled with `#0F172A`, text `#FFFFFF`, rounded `12px`, padding `14px 24px`. On hover, subtle lift with amber glow focus ring (`0 0 0 3px rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.25)`).
- **Secondary (Warm Outline):** Transparent background, `1.5px solid #0F172A`, text `#0F172A`. On hover, background shifts to `#F5F5F4`.
- **Destructive / Skip:** Subtle rose wash (`#FFF1F2`), text `#E11D48`, no outer border. Escalates on hover to `#FEE2E2`.

### 2. Introduction & Profile Cards
- Stacked card surfaces with physical swipe gestures. 
- Top third features photography framed by warm stone borders; middle third hosts the editorial name (`headline-lg` in Newsreader) alongside verified badge; bottom section features vouches rendered as mini blockquotes with the vouching member's avatar and credibility weight.

### 3. Vouch Badges & Chips
- **Verified Credential:** Emerald pill container (`#ECFDF5`), emerald text (`#065F46`), accompanied by a 12px solid check icon.
- **Vouch Count Pill:** Amber tint (`#FEF3C7`), amber text (`#92400E`), icon of interlocking hands or laurel leaf.
- **Interest / Community Chips:** `#F5F5F4` background, `#57534E` text, `6px 12px` padding, rounded `8px`.

### 4. Dual-Mode Switcher (Member vs. Voucher Mode)
- Segmented toggle anchored at the top navigation bar.
- Selected state for **Member** highlights clean slate (`#0F172A`) with white text.
- Selected state for **Voucher** activates an amber treatment (`#D97706`) with light amber ambient illumination, signaling responsibility for endorsing new community entrants.

### 5. Offline / Synced Queue Pill Indicator
- Floating pill indicator positioned top-center or bottom-left (`z-index: 40`).
- **Synced State:** `#ECFDF5` background, `1px solid #A7F3D0`, `#065F46` label with a micro pulsating emerald status dot.
- **Offline / Enqueued State:** `#FFFBEB` background, `1px solid #FDE68A`, `#92400E` label showing pending vouch count (e.g., `"3 vouches queued"`).

### 6. Dev Panel Floating Access Trigger
- Discreet, bottom-right floating trigger (`fixed bottom-6 right-6`).
- Deep charcoal pill (`#0F172A`), `border: 1px solid #334155`, text `#94A3B8`, mono-spaced small label (`JetBrains Mono` or `Plus Jakarta Sans` 11px uppercase).
- Expands into a slide-over panel permitting quick state switching (Network Latency simulation, Role swapping between Member and Voucher, and Mock Trust Graph alterations).

### 7. Form Inputs & Endorsement Textareas
- Surface `#FFFFFF` on `#FAFAF9` substrate, `1.5px solid #E7E5E4`, corner radius `12px`.
- Active focus transitions border to `#D97706` with an amber halo ring (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.15)`).
- Endorsement textareas incorporate a real-time character intentionality threshold (indicating "Thoughtful voucher depth reached" at 140+ characters).
